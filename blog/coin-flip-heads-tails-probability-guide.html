Heads or Tails: Math & Fun of Flipping a Coin Online

Flipping a coin is the oldest way to resolve disputes and demonstrate basic binary probability. Learn how online coin flips simulate actual physics.

🛠 TIP: Use our free Coin Flip Simulator to flip a coin with 3D animation or toss batches of coins to test percentages.

The Probability of a Coin Toss

Every standard coin flip is a classic 50% (1 in 2) probability event. However, in small samples (like flipping a coin 6 times), you might see 5 Heads and 1 Tail. This variance is completely normal.

Testing the Law of Large Numbers

According to the **Law of Large Numbers**, as you perform more trials, the actual percentage of outcomes will converge closer and closer to the expected theoretical probability of 50%. You can test this by running 500 coin tosses instantly inside our simulator.

How to Compress, Edit, and E-Sign PDF Documents Online Free

PDFs are the global standard for business documents, agreements, and certificates. Unfortunately, editing or signing them often requires downloading bloatware or paying expensive subscription fees. Here is how you can manage all your PDF workflows for free in your browser securely.

1. Draw or Upload Signatures Instantly

When you get an employment agreement or contract sent via email, printing it out just to sign it, scan it, and send it back is a huge waste of time. Electronic signatures have made paper signatures obsolete.

With our free E-Sign PDF Tool, you can upload any document, draw your signature using your mouse/touchpad, or upload an image of your signature to place it perfectly on any page of the PDF.

2. Add Watermarks to Protect Your Intellectual Property

If you are sharing sensitive business presentations, draft documents, or creative layouts, you should protect them from unauthorized distribution. Adding a custom watermark like "CONFIDENTIAL" or "DRAFT" is the best way to do this.

Our online Watermark PDF Tool lets you stamp customizable text across your documents with custom colors, angles, sizes, and transparency settings.

3. Combine or Reorganize Files with Merge & Split

Oftentimes, you have multiple scanned pages that you need to merge into a single cohesive document, or a huge document from which you only want to extract a couple of pages.

Instead of struggling with heavy software, you can drag and drop your files into our Merge & Split PDF Tool. You can combine multiple files in seconds or extract individual sheets as separate PDFs.

4. Shrink PDF File Size for Email Attachments

Many email clients and job portals reject attachments that exceed 5MB or 10MB. To compress your PDF without compromising legibility, you can run it through a smart compressor.

Our online browser-based PDF Compressor Tool reduces file size down to under 200KB or 500KB while preserving the crispness of the text and images inside.

🔒 Security Reminder: Never upload confidential contracts to websites that process PDFs on remote cloud servers. Our tools run locally in client-side Javascript. Your files are processed strictly inside your browser, meaning they never touch external servers.

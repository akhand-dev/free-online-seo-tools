How to Convert Timestamps, Timezones, and Generate Slugs & UTMs

Web development and digital marketing require executing dozens of tiny tasks daily. Converting database integer timestamps into human readable dates, planning online meetings across multiple timezones, formatting clean blog URLs, generating design placeholder text, and constructing campaign links are part of the job. Here is how to automate them.

1. Converting Unix Epoch Timestamps

Computers and database engines track time in Unix time (Epoch) — the total seconds since 1970. Reading database logs like 1785023900 with the naked eye is impossible.

Instead of writing quick script files, use our free Timestamp Converter Tool to instantly translate Unix numbers to UTC and local calendar dates (and vice versa).

2. Scheduling Calls across Global Timezones

Coordinating schedules for remote teams spread across New York, London, and New Delhi is notoriously tricky due to Daylight Saving Time offsets.

Our online Timezone Converter Tool lets you compare multiple international cities simultaneously, helping you find the perfect meeting slot without calculation errors.

3. Building Clean URL Slugs for SEO

Search engines prefer clean, readable URLs. A title like "My First Blog Post!" should be formatted as `my-first-blog-post`. Special characters, spaces, and capital letters need to be removed or replaced with hyphens.

Use our free browser Text to Slug Tool to sanitize any headline string into a clean URL slug instantly.

4. Generating Placeholder Layout Text

When prototyping website layouts or newsletter drafts, you need temporary text block fillers to check the design hierarchy before final copywriting is complete.

Our custom Lorem Ipsum Generator Tool outputs paragraph blocks, sentences, or word lists of dummy text in a single click.

5. Creating UTM Campaign Tracking Links

To analyze the effectiveness of your social media posts, newsletter banners, and paid advertising campaigns in Google Analytics, you must append tracking tags to your links.

Instead of typing them out and risking syntax breaks, use our interactive UTM Link Builder Tool. Simply fill in the website URL, campaign source, medium, and name, and copy the ready-to-use tracking link.

💡 Marketing Pro-Tip: Keep your UTM tags lowercase. Google Analytics treats `cpc` and `CPC` as two separate traffic mediums, which can split and clutter your reporting statistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

An Epoch (or Unix) timestamp is the total count of seconds (or milliseconds) that have elapsed since January 1, 1970 (UTC). It is used globally in database logs and APIs. You can translate them to human-readable dates using our Timestamp Converter.
A URL slug is the last part of a URL address. Clean, keyword-rich slugs tell search engines and users exactly what a page contains, boosting click-through rates and search ranking. You can clean strings into SEO slugs using our Text to Slug tool.
UTM (Urchin Tracking Module) parameters are tags added to a URL. When a user clicks the link, Google Analytics tracks the exact campaign source, medium, and term. You can build these tracking URLs easily using our UTM Link Builder.

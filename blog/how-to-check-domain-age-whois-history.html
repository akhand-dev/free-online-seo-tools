How to Check Domain Age & WHOIS Registration History

Analyzing domain registration schedules is essential for competitive research and domain brokers. Learn how to query WHOIS and calculate domain age.

🛠 TIP: Use our free Domain Age Checker to view registry creation events, updates, and expirations instantly.

Why Domain Age Matters in Web Projects

An older domain typically possesses built-in trust and backlink portfolios. By checking domain age and WHOIS updates, you can evaluate candidate domains for acquisition and research competitive authority roots.

How WHOIS and RDAP Protocols Deliver Registry Data

WHOIS databases record ownership and technical contact details. The modern replacement protocol, RDAP, delivers these properties in clean JSON objects, enabling calculators to calculate exact elapsed years, months, and days.

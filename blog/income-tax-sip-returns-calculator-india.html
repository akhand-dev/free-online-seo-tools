How to Calculate Income Tax & SIP Returns in India (2025-2026 Guide)

Managing taxes and planning long-term investments in India can feel overwhelming. With the recent updates to the Old and New tax regimes for FY 2025-26, it is crucial to make informed decisions. This guide will show you how to compare regimes easily and project your mutual fund returns using online calculators.

1. Old vs New Tax Regime: Which Should You Choose?

For the financial year 2025-26 (Assessment Year 2026-27), the Indian government continues to push taxpayers toward the New Tax Regime by offering lower tax slabs and standard deductions. However, the Old Tax Regime might still be optimal for you if you invest heavily in tax-saving instruments.

Key Deductions Available ONLY in the Old Regime:

  • Section 80C: Up to ₹1.5 Lakhs (PPF, ELSS mutual funds, LIC, EPF, etc.)
  • Section 80D: Medical insurance premiums for self and parents (up to ₹75,000)
  • Section 24(b): Home loan interest payments (up to ₹2 Lakhs)
  • HRA: House Rent Allowance exemptions

If you don't have these investments, the New Tax Regime is usually the simplest and most cost-effective path because it boasts lower tax rates across all brackets.

2. How to Plan Wealth Creation Using a SIP Calculator

Once you optimize your tax liabilities, the next step is building wealth. Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) in mutual funds are one of the most effective ways for retail investors in India to benefit from compounding.

Instead of trying to time the market, a SIP allows you to invest a fixed amount monthly, averaging out market volatility (rupee cost averaging).

How to Project Your Returns:

To estimate how much wealth you can accumulate over 5, 10, or 20 years, use our free online SIP Calculator India. Simply input:

  1. Your monthly investment amount (e.g., ₹5,000)
  2. Expected annual return rate (typically 12% - 15% for equity mutual funds over the long term)
  3. Time period in years

The calculator will instantly show your total invested amount, your estimated returns, and the final maturity wealth.

3. Calculating GST Online for Business Owners & Freelancers

If you run a business or work as a freelancer in India, you also have to manage the Goods and Services Tax (GST). Whether you need to add GST to an invoice or extract it from a total purchase price, doing the calculations manually is error-prone.

How to Check Keyword Density for Free: Complete SEO Guide

Crafting content that ranks on Google requires a careful balance of topical relevance and natural writing. In this tutorial, learn how to calculate keyword density, evaluate 1-3 word phrases, and protect your articles from keyword stuffing flags.

🛠 FREE TOOL: Test your article or webpage density in real-time with our Free Keyword Density Checker & Counter.

What is Keyword Density in SEO?

Keyword density measures the percentage of times a specific keyword appears in your text relative to the total number of words. It is computed using this mathematical formula:

Keyword Density (%) = (Number of Keyword Mentions / Total Word Count) × 100

For example, if your article is 1,000 words long and your target keyword appears 15 times, the keyword density is 1.5%.

The Recommended Keyword Density Benchmark

Search engines use Natural Language Processing (NLP) and TF-IDF algorithms to assess page context. Here are standard guidelines:

  • 1.0% to 2.0% (Ideal Sweet Spot): Provides strong topical signals to search engines without feeling repetitive to readers.
  • 0.5% to 1.0% (Long-Tail Phrases): Great for 2-word and 3-word combinations.
  • Above 3.0% (Keyword Stuffing Risk): When keywords are unnaturally repeated, search engines may trigger spam filters, pushing your rankings down.

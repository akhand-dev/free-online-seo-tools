How to Check Keyword Density for Free: SEO Optimization Guide

Writing SEO content requires balancing target search terms and natural phrasing. Learn how to calculate keyword density, filter structure words, and audit your articles using our free analyzer.

🛠 TIP: Use our free Keyword Density Checker to instantly extract copy from text or webpage URLs and view phrase metrics.

What is Keyword Density?

Keyword density indicates how frequently a specific keyword or phrase appears on a webpage relative to the total number of words. It is computed using this simple formula:

Density = (Keyword Occurrences / Total Words) * 100

The Ideal SEO Keyword Density Ranges

Modern search engines focus heavily on natural, user-centric writing. As a general benchmark:

  • Primary Keywords: Maintain a density between 1% and 2%.
  • Long-Tail Phrases: Keep density around 0.5% to 1%.
  • Maximum Limit: Going over 3% can look like keyword stuffing to crawler bots, risking organic rank drops.

Using Multi-Word Phrases (Bigrams & Trigrams)

Users rarely search for single words. Instead, they search using multi-word phrases (e.g., "best budget laptop"). When auditing, inspect phrase lengths:

  • 1-Word Keywords: Focus on topic-specific terms.
  • 2-Word Phrases: Target core search combinations.
  • 3-Word Phrases: Analyze natural conversational patterns.

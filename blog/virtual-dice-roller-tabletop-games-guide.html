How to Simulate Dice Rolls Online for Tabletop Games

Don't have physical dice for your board game session or RPG encounter? Learn how to roll virtual dice online, support DnD checks, and log roll history.

🛠 TIP: Use our free Dice Roller to roll D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, or D20 dice with interactive animations.

The Conveniences of Online Dice Rollers

Digital dice rollers are essential for remote tabletop campaigns (like DnD sessions over Discord or Zoom) and general board games, offering:

  • No Lost Pieces: Instantly roll multiple dice without losing them.
  • Instant Math: Get the exact sum of complex rolls instantly.
  • Transparency: View history logs of all previous rolls in the session.

Standard Polyhedral Dice for Roleplaying Games

Roleplaying games use a range of multi-sided dice. A typical set includes:

  • D6: The classic six-sided cube used in games like Monopoly and Yahtzee.
  • D20: The iconic 20-sided die used in D&D to check for actions, saving throws, and critical hits.

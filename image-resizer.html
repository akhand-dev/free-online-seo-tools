The Importance of Proper Image Resizing

Uploading raw photographs directly to web platforms can cause slow page speeds and formatting errors. Sizing your files correctly solves these issues:

  • Social Media Presets: Layout templates for platforms like Instagram enforce specific aspect ratios (e.g., 1:1 square or 9:16 story profiles) to keep cards sharp.
  • Government Portals (Passport Size): Identification platforms require small photo dimensions (e.g. 2 x 2 inches or 350x350 pixels) and small file limits (like 100KB).

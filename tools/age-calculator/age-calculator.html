How Is Your Age Calculated?

Our online Age Calculator computes the precise duration between two dates. It counts leap years, varying monthly day spans (28, 30, or 31 days), and time zone differences, translating your lifespan into years, months, weeks, days, and seconds.

How to Use the Date of Birth Calculator:

  1. Select your Date of Birth using the date input.
  2. Optionally configure the target date in the "Age at the Date of" field (defaults to today's local date).
  3. The results grid instantly populates, listing your exact age, next birthday countdown metrics, and total aggregate stats (total months, weeks, days, and hours lived).

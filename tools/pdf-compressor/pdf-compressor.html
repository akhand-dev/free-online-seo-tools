Reduce PDF File Size Online

PDF documents can grow significantly in file size when they contain multiple elements, high-resolution scans, embedded metadata, or duplicate resources. Our **PDF Compressor Online** provides two methods to clean and shrink PDF layouts directly in your browser. All computations are conducted locally on your computer, meaning your confidential financial reports, tax returns, and digital signatures remain completely private.

How to Compress a PDF Locally:

Optimize your files instantly in your browser:

  • Upload File: Drag and drop your target PDF file or click inside the folder icon to browse local files.
  • Choose Mode:
    • Standard (Safe Mode): Instantly strips out duplicate font definitions, system metadata headers, and compresses structural object streams. Excellent for text-heavy documents.
    • High (Rasterize Mode): Best for scanned files. This option renders each page to a clean canvas at a chosen resolution (e.g. 150 DPI) and bundles them into high-compression JPEGs.
  • Quality Adjustments: Fine-tune the JPEG quality slider. Lower values create smaller files, while higher values preserve image clarity.
  • Download Result: Once processing is complete, view the savings report (often saving up to 80% on scanned files) and save the document.

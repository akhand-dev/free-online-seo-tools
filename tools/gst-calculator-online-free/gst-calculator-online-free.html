How to Calculate GST (Adding vs. Removing)

Depending on your pricing system, you will either need to add GST to a base cost or extract GST from a final invoice amount. The formulas for these calculations are explained below:

1. Formula to Add GST (Tax Exclusive)

To calculate GST on a net price, multiply the base amount by the GST rate:

GST Amount = Base Price * (GST Rate / 100)
Total Price = Base Price + GST Amount

Example: For a net price of ₹10,000 and 18% GST: GST = ₹10,000 * 0.18 = ₹1,800. The gross price is ₹11,800.

2. Formula to Remove GST (Tax Inclusive)

To extract the base net price from a gross tax-inclusive total, use the following formula:

Base Price = Gross Price / (1 + (GST Rate / 100))
GST Amount = Gross Price - Base Price

Example: For a gross total of ₹11,800 containing 18% GST: Base Price = ₹11,800 / 1.18 = ₹10,000. The GST component is ₹1,800.

CGST and SGST Divisions

For sales within the same state (intra-state transactions) in India, the total GST is split equally between the Central Government and the State Government. These components are termed:

  • CGST (Central GST): The revenue share allocated to the central treasury.
  • SGST (State GST): The revenue share allocated to the state treasury.

For example, if the GST rate is 18%, CGST is calculated at 9% and SGST at 9% of the net price. Our calculator calculates these breakdowns automatically, helping you write invoices accurately.

GST Slabs in India

India's GST structure features five primary tax rates:

  1. 0% (Nil Rate): Essential goods like food items and raw agricultural produce.
  2. 5%: Common items like packaged foods, garments, and basic transportation services.
  3. 12%: Business services, processed foods, and mid-range consumer goods.
  4. 18%: The standard tax slab applying to most software services, corporate fees, restaurants, and industrial goods.
  5. 28%: Premium goods, luxury items, automobiles, and sin-tax products.

The Mathematics of PDF Layout Rendering

PDF documents are structured in a vector format, containing font metrics, layout grids, and embedded graphics. When you convert a PDF page to a JPG, a renderer calculates coordinates and maps vector graphics to pixel bitmaps. Sizing up the scale selects how many dots per inch (DPI) are created, resulting in crisp output images.

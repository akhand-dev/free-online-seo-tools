How Client-Side Paraphrasing Works

Many online rewriters send your text to remote AI servers, where it is analyzed. This can be slow, requires an active internet connection, and raises privacy issues. Our tool operates completely locally using an offline dictionary in JavaScript. The tool does the following:

  1. Word Parsing: Tokenizes your text into individual sentences and words.
  2. Synonym Mapping: Scans the words against a local dictionary containing thousands of synonyms (for verbs, adjectives, nouns, and transitions).
  3. Contextual Substitution: Replaces matched words with synonyms based on your selected mode (Standard, Formal, Creative). Capitalization is preserved.
  4. Interactive Selection: Highlights the rewritten words in the output panel. You can click on any highlighted word to select other synonyms or toggle back to the original word.

Core Techniques for Effective Paraphrasing

To rewrite your text successfully while maintaining the original meaning:

  • Restructure Sentences: Swap active and passive voices (e.g., change "The team completed the project" to "The project was completed by the team").
  • Use Varied Synonyms: Replace common verbs like "use" with formal alternatives like "utilize" or "employ."
  • Split Long Sentences: Break complex sentences into shorter, clearer statements to improve readability.

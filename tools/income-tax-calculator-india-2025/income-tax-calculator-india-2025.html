Understanding the Income Tax Slabs under the New Tax Regime

Our online Income Tax Calculator India New Regime tool is specifically structured to calculate your taxes under the default tax layout of FY 2025-26 (Assessment Year 2026-27). Following the updates in budget revisions, the New Tax Regime is the default selection in India. It offers lower slab rates compared to the Old Regime, though it requires giving up most traditional exemptions like Section 80C, 80D, and HRA.

How Slabs Progressive Taxation Works

Income tax in India is computed progressively. Instead of taxing your entire income at a single rate, your taxable income is divided into slabs, with tax rates increasing only on the income within that specific slab. Under the New Tax Regime, the slabs are structured as follows:

  • Up to ₹3,00,000: Nil (No tax)
  • ₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000: 5%
  • ₹7,00,001 to ₹10,00,000: 10%
  • ₹10,00,001 to ₹12,00,000: 15%
  • ₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000: 20%
  • Above ₹15,00,000: 30%

The Standard Deduction Advantage in 2025

Salaried individuals receive an automatic Standard Deduction of ₹75,000 under the New Regime. This deduction is directly subtracted from your gross salary, reducing your taxable income block. If you are self-employed or operate as a sole proprietor, you do not receive standard deduction benefit, and your tax is calculated based on gross professional business receipts.

Section 87A Rebate Explained

Section 87A provides complete relief to middle-income earners in India. Under the New Regime, if your taxable income after standard deduction does not exceed ₹7,00,000, your tax is fully rebated. This means that a salaried employee earning a gross CTC of up to ₹7,75,000 will pay ₹0 tax (₹7,75,000 gross minus ₹75,000 standard deduction equals ₹7,00,000 taxable income, which triggers the rebate).

Detailed Example of Tax Calculation

Suppose you earn a gross salary of ₹10,00,000. Here is how your progressive tax is calculated under the New Tax Regime:

  1. Gross Income = ₹10,00,000
  2. Subtract Standard Deduction = ₹75,000
  3. Taxable Income = ₹9,25,000
  4. Slab Calculations:
    • Slab 1 (0 to 3L): Nil
    • Slab 2 (3L to 7L): 5% of ₹4,00,000 = ₹20,000
    • Slab 3 (7L to 9.25L): 10% of ₹2,25,000 = ₹22,500
  5. Tax Before Cess = ₹20,000 + ₹22,500 = ₹42,500
  6. Health & Education Cess (4%) = 4% of ₹42,500 = ₹1,700
  7. Total Income Tax Payable: ₹42,500 + ₹1,700 = ₹44,200

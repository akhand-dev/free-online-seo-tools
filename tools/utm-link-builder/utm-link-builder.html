What is a Campaign Link Generator?

A campaign link generator is an indispensable tool for digital marketers and social media managers. When you share a link to your website in an email newsletter, a Facebook ad, or a YouTube description, you need to know exactly how many people clicked it and which specific campaign drove them to your site. By using our tool to append UTM (Urchin Tracking Module) parameters, you can send detailed tracking data directly to Google Analytics 4 (GA4).

How to Use the Google UTM Link Generator

Our tool url builder makes creating error-free tracking links simple:

  • Enter Your URL: Start by pasting the full destination link (e.g., `https://www.yoursite.com/sale`).
  • Define the Source: The utm_source identifies the advertiser or site sending traffic (e.g., `google`, `newsletter`, `twitter`).
  • Set the Medium: The utm_medium specifies the advertising or marketing medium (e.g., `cpc`, `banner`, `email`).
  • Name the Campaign: The utm_campaign groups your overall efforts (e.g., `spring_sale_2026`).
  • Optional Tags: Use utm_term for paid search keywords and utm_content to differentiate between similar links.

Why Use an Automated UTM String Builder?

Manually typing out tracking URLs is highly prone to typos, which can completely break your Google Analytics reporting. A UTM string builder automatically formats, combines, and URL-encodes your parameters into a perfect query string (e.g., ?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc). It ensures your links are structurally sound, spaces are correctly escaped as `%20`, and special characters won't break the browser.

How Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Works

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology analyzes the patterns of light and dark pixels on an image file to isolate shapes representing letters, symbols, numbers, and grammar styles, parsing them into digital characters.

Key Benefits of Client-Side OCR:

  • Ultimate Privacy: Tesseract.js loads language weights into your browser's local sandbox, executing translations locally. None of your invoices, documents, or personal letters are ever transmitted to external APIs.
  • High Accuracy: Recognizes standard book fonts, document layouts, and screenshot formats with accurate line splits.

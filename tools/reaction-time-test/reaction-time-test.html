How Visual Reaction Time Works

Your **visual reaction time** represents the amount of time that passes between a visual stimulus appearing on the screen (the box turning green) and your motor sensory response (clicking the mouse or tapping the touchpad).

  • The average human reaction time is approximately **273 milliseconds**.
  • Reflex speeds are influenced by age, sleep hygiene, attention focus, and physical fatigue.
  • Gamers and esports athletes often achieve reaction latencies of **150 to 200 milliseconds** through continuous practice.

How to Improve Scores:

To record accurate times, try using a desktop mouse rather than a laptop touchpad, since touchpads add mechanical tap delay. Additionally, testing on a high-refresh-rate monitor (144Hz or 240Hz) reduces display lag, allowing the green pixels to draw faster than on standard 60Hz panels.

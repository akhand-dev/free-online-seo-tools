Understanding GST Calculations in India

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a unified, multi-stage, destination-based tax system introduced in India in 2017 to replace multiple indirect taxes. It splits tax collections into:

  • CGST (Central GST): Collected by the Central Government on intra-state supply.
  • SGST (State GST): Collected by the State Government on intra-state supply.
  • IGST (Integrated GST): Collected by the Central Government on inter-state transactions.

Gold GST Tax Structure:

Gold purchasing behaves differently than general goods. Standard Gold GST is 3%, which applies to both the gold value and making charges. An additional 5% GST applies to making charges if jewelry crafting services are billed separately, but typically the standard rate covers the invoice value.

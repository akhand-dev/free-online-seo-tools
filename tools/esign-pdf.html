How to eSign PDF Documents Free

Signing contracts, tax returns, non-disclosure agreements, and digital forms should be fast and simple. Our online **eSign PDF** tool allows you to place electronic signatures on your documents locally in your browser. Since no file uploads are transmitted to external cloud systems, your private details and signature designs are 100% secure.

How to Draw or Type Electronic Signatures:

  • Upload PDF: Click to load the document from your local folders.
  • Adopt Signature:
    • Draw Mode: Draw your signature using a stylus, mouse, or touch pad. Click "Adopt Signature" to save it.
    • Type Mode: Enter your name into the box, watch the cursive script generator render the design, and adopt the text.
    • Upload Mode: Import a pre-rendered image of your signature (transparent PNG files work best).
  • Drag and Drop Placement: Click "Place Signature". Drag the box on the PDF viewport to position it precisely (e.g. on the signer signature line). Resize the signature box using the circular corner handles.
  • Download Signed Document: Tap "Sign & Download PDF" to stitch the signature image into the PDF pages using relative coordinate transforms, saving the signed file instantly.

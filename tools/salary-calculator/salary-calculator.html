Difference Between CTC and In-Hand Salary

Your CTC (Cost to Company) is the gross annual expenditure a company spends on an employee. It is not equivalent to your monthly take-home salary due to statutory contributions and taxes:

  • Basic Salary: Core salary base (usually 40%-50% of CTC).
  • EPF Deductions: 12% of basic salary is saved into the Employee Provident Fund.
  • Professional Tax: A state-level direct tax (generally capped around â‚¹200 monthly).

The Mathematics of Gross vs Net Salary

Annual cost-to-company (CTC) structures differ significantly from take-home liquid cash. Deductions for pensions and tax structures must be deducted to find net values:

  • Gross Salary: Total annual compensation package before any pension or tax declarations.
  • Net Take-Home: The actual liquid currency deposited in your banking accounts monthly after mandatory deductions.

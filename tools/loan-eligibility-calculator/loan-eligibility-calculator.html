How Banks Check Loan Eligibility

Lenders evaluate your borrowing capacity using the FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) parameter. Typically, banks restrict total debt commitments (existing EMIs + proposed EMI) to 50% of net monthly income to prevent default risks.

How to Improve Loan Eligibility:

  • Pay off smaller credit card balances or personal loans to reduce existing EMIs.
  • Opt for a longer tenure to decrease the monthly EMI burden, thereby raising eligible principal bounds.
  • Add a co-borrower (spouse/parent) to combine income streams.

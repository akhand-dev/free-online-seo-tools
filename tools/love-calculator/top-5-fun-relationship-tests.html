Top 5 Fun Relationship Tests to Try with Your Partner or Friends

Whether you're three months into a new romance, celebrating a five-year anniversary, or hanging out on a Friday night with a group of close friends, there's one thing that never fails to spark laughter: interactive relationship tests.

From viral social media personality filters and late-night dating quizzes to playful matchmaking tools, humans love finding new ways to measure their connections. A great relationship game isn't meant to be taken dead seriously. Instead, it serves as a fantastic icebreaker, an excuse to ask playful questions you might not normally bring up, and a guaranteed source of shared laughter.

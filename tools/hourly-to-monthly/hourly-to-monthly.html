How to Convert Hourly Wage to Monthly Salary

Translating hourly wages into stable monthly or annual packages follows a standard payroll equation:

Annual Salary = Hourly Wage * Hours per Week * 52 Weeks

To find the monthly equivalent, divide the annual product by 12:

Monthly Salary = Annual Salary / 12

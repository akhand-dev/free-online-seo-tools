How to Remove Image Backgrounds Offline

Our online Background Remover utilizes your local browser Canvas API. This guarantees that your private images, product mockups, and corporate portraits never leave your computer or get uploaded to remote web servers.

How to Use the Magic Wand & Color Key:

  • 🪄 Magic Wand: Select this tool, set your tolerance slider (higher tolerance removes a wider range of similar colors), and click on any portion of the image background. The tool instantly flood-fills connected pixels, making them transparent.
  • 🎨 Color Keyer: Choose a specific backdrop color using the color selector and click "Remove Color" to delete all matching pixels across the entire canvas simultaneously (perfect for white product backdrops or green-screen backgrounds).

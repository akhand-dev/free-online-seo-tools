What is an SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a facility offered by mutual funds to help investors save regularly. An SIP allows you to allocate a fixed sum of money at pre-defined intervals (monthly, quarterly) to your chosen mutual fund scheme.

compounded SIP Growth Formula:

The mathematical equation used to calculate future wealth gained is:

FV = P × [ ( (1 + i)^n - 1 ) / i ] × (1 + i)

Where:

  • FV: Future value of wealth.
  • P: Monthly investment amount.
  • i: Monthly interest rate (expected annual yield % divided by 12 × 100).
  • n: Total number of monthly installments.

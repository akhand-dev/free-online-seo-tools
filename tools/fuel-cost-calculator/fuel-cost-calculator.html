How to Calculate Trip Fuel Consumption

Planning road trip expenditures requires understanding vehicle gas mileage and fuel metrics. Depending on your region, fuel measurements apply different units:

The Formulas:

  1. Imperial Mode (US/UK - Miles & MPG):
    Calculates fuel needed in Gallons:
    Fuel Needed (Gallons) = Trip Distance (Miles) / Fuel Efficiency (MPG)
    Total Cost = Fuel Needed * Price per Gallon
  2. Metric Mode (EU/Rest of World - Km & L/100km):
    Calculates fuel needed in Liters:
    Fuel Needed (Liters) = (Trip Distance (Km) * Fuel Efficiency (L/100km)) / 100
    Total Cost = Fuel Needed * Price per Liter

How to convert MPG to L/100km:

If you need to translate efficiency rates:
L/100km = 235.21 / MPG (US Gallons)
L/100km = 282.48 / MPG (UK Gallons)

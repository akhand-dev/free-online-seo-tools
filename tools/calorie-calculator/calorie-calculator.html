The Science of Weight Deficit

Our calorie calculator utilizes the Miffln-St Jeor formula, widely recognized in clinical nutrition as the most accurate standard for calculating BMR:

  • Men BMR: 10 * weight (kg) + 6.25 * height (cm) - 5 * age (y) + 5
  • Women BMR: 10 * weight (kg) + 6.25 * height (cm) - 5 * age (y) - 161

Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) is then calculated by multiplying your BMR by your physical activity modifier.

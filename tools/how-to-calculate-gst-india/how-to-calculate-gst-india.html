GST Compliance and Rules in India

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a unified multi-stage consumption tax levied on goods and services in India. Understanding the tax splits is essential for business operators:

1. Intrastate Transactions (Within State)

When selling to a buyer in the same state, the tax is split equally into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST).

2. Interstate Transactions (Across States)

When selling to a buyer in a different state, a single unified tax called IGST (Integrated GST) is levied.

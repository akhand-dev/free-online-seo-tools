The US Navy Circumference Method

Body fat ratios are important indicators of cardiovascular health and general fitness levels. This calculator uses equations developed by the US Navy Medical Research Center to calculate percentages based on neck, waist, and hip boundaries:

  • Male: 495 / ( 1.0324 - 0.19077 * log10(waist - neck) + 0.15456 * log10(height) ) - 450
  • Female: 495 / ( 1.29579 - 0.35004 * log10(waist + hip - neck) + 0.22100 * log10(height) ) - 450

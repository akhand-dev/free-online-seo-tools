Free Typing Speed Test (WPM Checker)

Developing keyboard fluency is a critical skill for developers, content creators, copywriters, and indexing administrators. Our free **Typing Speed Test Online** allows you to test your characters-per-minute speed and error calculations. Featuring minimalist aesthetic frames, customizable duration intervals, and local leaderboard integrations, you can practice your speed offline safely.

How WPM Speed is Computed:

Our simulator calculates scores based on standard industry formulas:

  • Gross WPM: Calculated by dividing total typed character keystrokes by 5 (standard word length), then dividing by time interval in minutes: Gross WPM = (Total Keystrokes / 5) / (Seconds / 60).
  • Net WPM: Accounts for accuracy by stripping error strikes: Net WPM = Gross WPM - (Uncorrected Errors / Minutes).
  • Accuracy Rate %: Represents the ratio of correctly typed key elements compared to total stroke inputs: Accuracy = (Correct Characters / Total Keystrokes) * 100.

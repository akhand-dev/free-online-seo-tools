The simple interest Formula

Simple interest is determined by multiplying the daily interest rate by the principal by the number of days that elapse between payments. The formula is:

Simple Interest = P * R * T / 100

Where:

  • P: Principal investment sum.
  • R: Yearly interest rate.
  • T: Time duration in years.

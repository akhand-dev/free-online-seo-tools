Password Strength Checker

Analyze the security strength of your credentials locally in your browser. No password data is ever sent to a server.

Strength Score: Too Weak
  • ❌ Minimum 8 characters
  • ❌ At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • ❌ At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
  • ❌ At least one numeric digit (0-9)
  • ❌ At least one special symbol (@, #, $, %, etc.)

Understanding Password Complexity Rules

Weak passwords are vulnerable to dictionary attacks and automated brute-force scripts. Strong password keys combine letters, numbers, and symbols in non-standard patterns, achieving high entropy limits.

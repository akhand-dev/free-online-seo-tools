Interactive Decision Spinner Wheel

Stuck between choosing dinner menus, assigning lottery numbers, picking names out of a hat, or deciding whether to finalize a project task? Our online **Spin the Wheel** decision maker builds physics-based spins right in your local browser. Equipped with custom slices, HSL color palettes, and retro tick audio clicks, making decisions is clean and fully private.

How Spin Slices Decelerate:

Our wheel physics script mimics standard kinetic rotations:

  • Initial Velocity: Triggering a spin sets a high angular velocity (typically rotating multiple full loops rapidly).
  • Kinetic Deceleration: A friction coefficient (e.g. 0.98 slowdown rate) multiplies the speed value inside a requestAnimationFrame frame queue, creating a smooth deceleration.
  • Sound Synthesis: The page uses a local **Web Audio API** oscillator synth to click-click on boundary intersections (pin passes).
  • Landing Calculations: When velocity zeroes out, the script maps the final rotation angle directly to the matching wedge boundary at the 12 o'clock needle marker.

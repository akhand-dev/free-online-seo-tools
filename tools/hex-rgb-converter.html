Understanding HEX and RGB Color Codes

Web styles and graphical assets utilize different alphanumeric formulas to express colors. Programmers typically reference **HEX values** (hexadecimal notation starting with a pound sign) in CSS files, while photo editing, native software, and UI design suites often display **RGB (Red, Green, Blue) coordinates**. Our free online **HEX ↔ RGB Converter** lets you instantly swap color codes dynamically inside your web browser.

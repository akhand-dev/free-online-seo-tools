Free B2B Invoice Generator Online

Drafting customer bills, calculating project costs, and generating clean invoice ledger breakdowns shouldn't require subscribing to accounting programs or using slow web templates that inject developer watermarks. Our free **Invoice Generator** compiles professional invoices locally. By entering quantities and discount parameters, grand totals compute instantly. GST Compliant Invoice Creation

For Indian freelancers and businesses, standard taxation structures apply. By checking **Enable GST Options**, you can include your business's GSTIN and the client's GSTIN. Under CGST/SGST treatment, taxes divide equally between central and state buckets, while interstate transactions display a single unified IGST tax row. Optional HSN/SAC code lists can be specified on each item ledger row.

How to Save Invoices as PDFs:

  • Fill Details: Input company logo headers, billing locations, due dates, and tax settings.
  • Add Line Items: Type item names, rates, and quantities. Subtotals sum automatically.
  • Print / Export: Click **Save PDF / Print**. In the destination menu select "Save as PDF", choosing portrait layout and standard scales.

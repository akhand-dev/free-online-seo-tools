What is a CPS Click Speed Test?

CPS (Clicks Per Second) measures the frequency at which you can press your mouse button in one second. It is a popular benchmark tool used by gamers to evaluate speed parameters for fast-paced action games. Standard clicking methods include:

  • Regular Clicking: Standard clicking using finger muscles. Normal scores average 4 to 6 CPS.
  • Jitter Clicking: Shaking your forearm and hand muscles to tap the mouse button rapidly. Standard scores average 7 to 10 CPS.
  • Butterfly Clicking: Alternate tap clicks using two fingers on a single mouse key. Yields speeds of 10 to 15+ CPS.

How Click Per Second is Calculated:

The mathematical formulation is simple. Divide the total sum of clicks recorded by the elapsed time duration:
CPS = Total Click Cycles / Test Time (Seconds)

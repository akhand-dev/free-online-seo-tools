Old vs New Tax Regime India

India provides two options to calculate and pay income taxes:

  • New Tax Regime: Features lower tax rates but does not allow most deductions (like Section 80C, 80D, HRA). The Standard Deduction is ₹75,000.
  • Old Tax Regime: Higher tax rates but lets you reduce your taxable income using multiple sections (80C up to 1.5L, 80D medical insurance, HRA housing allowances, etc.). Standard Deduction is ₹50,000.

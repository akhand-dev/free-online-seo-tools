How Is EMI Calculated?

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are applied to both interest and principal each month, so that over a specified number of years, the loan is fully paid off.

The EMI Mathematical Formula:

The mathematical formula for calculating EMI is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)

Where:

  • P: Principal Loan Amount.
  • r: Monthly Interest Rate (annual rate divided by 12 × 100).
  • n: Loan tenure in number of months.

The Mathematics of Loan Repayment

EMIs are computed using a reducing balance method. The formula to calculate Equated Monthly Installment values is:

EMI = P * r * (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)

  • r: Monthly Interest Rate (annual rate / 12 / 100).
  • n: Repayment Tenure in months.

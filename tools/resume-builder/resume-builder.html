Free Resume Builder Online

Creating job-winning resumes and curriculum vitaes shouldn't require paying subscription fees or uploading private profile details to automated harvesting platforms. Our free **Resume Builder** provides a clean drafting utility. By configuring profiles and experience listings, you can compile A4 templates and export them as clean vector PDFs locally using your browser's native printing overlay.

How to Save Your CV as a PDF:

  • Fill Forms: Complete your contact coordinates, job title, summary, employment items, and education records.
  • Choose Themes: Toggle between "Classic Professional" (with Playfair serif titles) and "Modern Minimalist" (with indigo accent markers) styles.
  • Verify Layout: Check the live sheet preview to ensure it matches within single-page boundary lines.
  • Download PDF: Click **Save PDF / Print**. In the browser print dialog screen, set the destination printer option to "Save as PDF", choose portrait orientations, check margins, and save.

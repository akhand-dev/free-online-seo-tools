How to Calculate Discounts

Our Discount Calculator helps shoppers, merchants, and sales reps figure out the final price of an item after store reductions and local tax additions are applied.

How Double (Stacked) Discounts Are Computed:

When a store offers an "extra 10% off" on top of an existing 20% sale price, these percentages are not added together (e.g. they do not equal 30% off). Instead, they are calculated sequentially:

  1. Apply the primary 20% discount to the original price.
  2. Apply the additional 10% discount to that newly reduced sale price.
  3. Add the local sales tax to obtain the final billing invoice.

The Mathematics of Discount Calculations

Determining promotional values ensures you purchase goods at optimized costs. The basic formula is:

Sale Price = Original Price * (1 - Discount / 100)

If sales tax overrides apply, multiply the discounted price by the tax parameter:

Final Price = Sale Price * (1 + Tax / 100)

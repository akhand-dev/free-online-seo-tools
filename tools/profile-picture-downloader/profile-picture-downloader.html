Save Instagram & TikTok PFPs in High Resolution

Backing up design avatars, saving customer profile icons for internal CRM layouts, or extracting high-resolution profile graphics from TikTok and Instagram shouldn't expose you to account login requests. Our online **Profile Picture Downloader** handles queries via unavatar proxy endpoints client-side. The picture resolves in seconds directly inside your browser.

How to Save PFPs:

  • Enter User Handle: Type their username (the `@` symbol is automatically handled and cleaned).
  • Load Avatar: Click **Get PFP**. The tool queries the proxy server and displays the high-res image.
  • Save: Tap **Open Profile Picture** to display the direct image, then save it to your folders.

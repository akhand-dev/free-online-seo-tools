How to Generate and Scan QR Codes Online

Our dual QR Code Generator & Scanner makes creating and decoding 2D barcodes simple. The utility compiles all instructions locally, making it 100% private. Your camera feed and files are never sent to external servers.

How to Scan a QR Code:

To scan a QR code, you can use either your device's camera or upload an image file:

  • Using Camera: Click "Start Camera", grant camera permissions when prompted, and point your webcam or mobile camera at the QR code. The scanner will automatically detect and extract the content.
  • Using File Upload: Click the file upload container, select a photo or screenshot of a QR code from your local files, and the scanner will decode it instantly.

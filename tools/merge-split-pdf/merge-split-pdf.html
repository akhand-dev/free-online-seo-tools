Combine and Extract PDF Files Offline

Managing large batches of PDF pages shouldn't require installing expensive desk apps or uploading document data to untrustworthy remote server pipelines. Our online **Merge & Split PDF** utility executes all page compilation operations inside your browser tab. Easily combine multiple draft versions into a finalized presentation, or separate large scanning batches into distinct, manageable files safely.

How to Merge PDF Files:

  • Select the **Merge PDFs** tab.
  • Browse or drag-and-drop multiple PDF files.
  • Reorder documents by clicking the Move Up/Down controls to align them in sequence.
  • Click **Merge PDFs** to instantly download the unified document.

How to Split PDF Pages:

  • Select the **Split PDF** tab.
  • Upload the target document. The system counts the pages automatically.
  • Choose between extracting a specific range (e.g. 2-5, 8 to bundle pages 2, 3, 4, 5, and 8 together) or extracting all pages into separate individual PDFs.
  • If separating all pages, the tool packs them into a single local **ZIP archive** for clean exports.

Add Watermarks to PDF Sheets Online

Stamping labels (like "COPY", "DRAFT", or corporate trademarks) over documents is standard practice to restrict usage, mark versions, or indicate drafts. Our free online **Add Watermark to PDF** tool overlays styling elements on your sheets locally. Operating purely in-browser, it protects sensitive files from server-side exposure.

How to Watermark Your PDF:

  • Upload the document. The page preview panel renders the cover page automatically.
  • Choose between **Text** and **Image** overlays.
  • Customize text formatting: type terms, choose Helvetica or Times New Roman fonts, choose colors, set rotations (e.g. 45 degrees), and set opacity levels (e.g. 30% to keep background text readable).
  • Use **Tiled Grid** options to repeat text arrays over the entire page body, preventing simple cropping.
  • For images, upload PNG or JPEG stamps, scale them, and place them centered or in margins.
  • Click **Apply Watermark** to run the local compiler and download the stamped document.

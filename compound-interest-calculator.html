The compound interest Formula

Unlike simple interest, compound interest accrues over time on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest. The formula is:

A = P * (1 + r/n)^(n*t)

Where:

  • A: Total final balance maturity value.
  • P: Principal investment sum.
  • r: Yearly interest rate.
  • n: Compounding frequency.
  • t: Time period in years.
